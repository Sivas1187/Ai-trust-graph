# Crosswalks: OWASP Agentic Top 10 and NIST AI RMF

> **Status: non-normative, indicative navigation.** Per [Artifact #5 §0.11](../docs/05-master-control-library.md), crosswalks support navigation and evidence reuse. They do **not** establish one-to-one equivalence, compliance, legal applicability or certification. A listed AI Trust Graph control is *relevant to* the item, not proof that the item is addressed; that still depends on evidence for the specific system.

## Reading the crosswalks

- ATG is designed for risk that comes from **how components connect**, for example identity and delegation (ASI03), tool authority (ASI02), cascading paths (ASI08) and mapping risks across all components (MAP 4). Whether it finds such risk reliably in practice is not yet validated (Artifact #10 B.4 is pending).
- ATG is deliberately narrower than NIST AI RMF: it does not address fairness, explainability, workforce diversity or benefits analysis, and should sit alongside a broader AI risk programme rather than replace one.
