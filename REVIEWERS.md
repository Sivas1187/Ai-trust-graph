# Reviewers

This file credits people who have reviewed AI Trust Graph. See [REVIEWING.md](REVIEWING.md) for how to take part.

**What a listing means:** the person contributed a review, and the findings are linked. It does **not** mean they endorse, approve or agree with the methodology, and it is not certification or peer review in any formal sense. Affiliations are shown only when the reviewer asks for them and are for identification; reviews are in a personal capacity unless stated otherwise.

**How names get here:** only with the reviewer's consent, in the form they choose. A reviewer can ask to be renamed or removed at any time.
